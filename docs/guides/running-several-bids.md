# Running several bids

It's Tuesday and three bids are due this week. Each bid is its own project: its
own rows, its own labor rate, its own tax rate, its own job details. The name of
the one you're in sits in the header, with the time of the last save under it.

Click the name to open the switcher. Everything on this page starts there.

## Start a bid

1. Click the job name in the header → **New project**.
2. Type the job's name and press **Create** (or Enter).

The dialog closes as soon as you press Create and the header changes to the new
name. If the bid you were on was still blank — never typed in — it is simply
given the name instead of being left behind as clutter.

> **What you'll see:** name a job while a bid with work on it is open and you end
> up with two bids in the list. Do it on the boot bid you never touched and you
> end up with one.

## Move between bids

Click the name and pick another bid. The list shows every live bid, the open one
pinned at the top with a dot beside it, and each one's age — *just now*, *today*,
*3d ago*. Everything comes back exactly: rows, components, the quantity you were
halfway through changing, the rate.

If you are inside a Devices, Conduit or Wire editor with unsaved work, the app
asks before it leaves — *"Discard unsaved changes in this editor?"* Cancel and you
stay in the editor on the same bid; OK and it leaves the editor first, then
changes bids. You never land on a blank page, and a row typed on one bid can
never be saved into another.

> **What you'll see:** with 24 bids in the list, the switcher scrolls and
> **Rename / New project / Manage projects…** stay pinned at the bottom where you
> can reach them.

## Rename

Click the name → **Rename**. The name becomes a field with the current text
selected; type over it and press Enter. The header updates in place. Renaming a
bid does **not** move it to the top of the list — a name is not an edit.

## Duplicate last month's job as a template

Click the name → **Manage projects…** → **Duplicate** on the row you want.

The copy is a deep copy: same rows, same rate, same details, independent from the
first keystroke. You stay on the original; open the copy from the same dialog.

> **What you'll see:** duplicating *Northgate Retail* twice gives you **Northgate
> Retail 2** and **Northgate Retail 3** — a numbered sibling, not a bid whose name
> is a note to yourself.

## Kill a dead bid

In **Manage projects…**, the table lists every bid under **Name**, **Lines** and
**Last edited**, and each row offers Open, Rename, Duplicate, Archive and Delete.

Delete asks inside the row, not in a browser pop-up, and says how much goes with
it. The bid you have open can't be deleted; its Delete button is greyed with the
reason on it — open another bid first.

> **What you'll see:** *Delete "Bravo" and its 3 lines? This can't be undone.*
> with **Delete** and **Cancel** beside it. **Lines** counts components too, so a
> run with two parts under it is 3.

## Archive the ones that are over

A bid you are done with does not have to be deleted. Archive it and it leaves the
switcher without leaving the app: the list shows your live bids and a line
underneath reading **"N archived"**, which opens them. Unarchive one and it comes
straight back. As with delete, you can't archive the bid you have open.

Inside **Manage projects…** the archived ones live in an **Archived (N)** block
you can fold shut, and the footer counts both — *2 live · 1 archived*.

## A bid a colleague sent you

Open their link and their bid arrives as a new bid of your own, at **their** labor
rate and tax rate, with their job details. Nothing of yours is touched or
replaced.

> **What you'll see:** a line under the header reading *"Copy of Northgate Retail,
> shared Sep 6 by link — edits stay on this device."* Dismiss it when you've read
> it. Open the same link again and you get that copy back — *"Reopened your copy
> of…"* — not a second one.

If they send a fresh link later, after more work, that one is a separate bid and
lands beside the first as **Northgate Retail 2**. Rename whichever one you keep,
so the two of you are not talking about different sheets of paper.

## Good to know

- The header's saved line is the real one: **Saved just now**, **Saved 2 min
  ago**, then *today* / *yesterday* once it's older. There is no Save button —
  see [Saving and signing in](saving-and-signing-in.md).
- A new bid starts at the labor rate of the bid you were in. That is usually your
  shop rate and usually right — but check it on the first job of a new client.
- The tax rate is per bid too, and starts at 8.5%.
- Undo does not cross bids. Switching bids clears the undo stack, so you cannot
  undo your way into another job's history.
- Long names keep their tail: *…Level 3 East Wing* and *…Level 3 West Wing* read
  as two different bids in the list, and the full name is on the hover.
- The list of bids lives on this device. Signing in makes the same bids appear on
  another computer — see [Saving and signing in](saving-and-signing-in.md).
- Deleting a bid on one computer deletes it on the others too, once they sync.
